# Forward consensus paper pilot v1

Registered before tonight's games at 2026-10-21 20:23 UTC. Machine-readable terms:
`forward-consensus-v1.json`. Status is **registered, not implemented or validated**.

Hypothesis: a fresh, exact-contract offered price with positive value under every
retained leave-offered-operator-out reference estimate may support positive
hypothetical returns after price/cost stress. Reference agreement is not proof of
independence or accurate fair value. This pilot tests that hypothesis prospectively.

Use the existing 3 percentage-point edge, 4% point-EV, -150 odds floor and 15-minute
freshness thresholds; these were not fitted to the first four observed outcomes.
Require at least two distinct reference operators, excluding offer/operator aliases.
Minimum-reference EV must be strictly positive. Full-game half-point spreads/totals
only, NFL and NBA separately; no reused NFL probabilities for basketball.

For each event, use the first complete authorized capture 30 minutes to six hours
before start after registration. Require reviewed identity, rules, source times,
eligible offer and availability evidence. Log missing evidence and abstain. Capture
timing is an operational sampling constraint, not a simulated real-time opportunity.
Do not backdate eligibility or substitute a later better price when the first
complete snapshot fails. A skipped event is a recorded coverage gap.

Select at most one contract per event: highest minimum-reference EV, then highest
point EV, then lexicographic exact contract/book identity. Across books, the same
selection is one contract. Retain all candidate/rejection evidence privately, plus
an immutable selection timestamp/policy hash. No selected-entry return may be
computed until exact final and observation-linked operator rules are verified.
Until policy tagging is implemented and verified, no row is a pilot selection.

Measure hypothetical fixed one-unit quote returns, with zero fixed-cost baseline
and 1%/2% per-unit cost stress. These assumptions do not establish accepted fills,
limits, fees or realized user P&L. Price watches/conditional props and earlier
retrospective rows remain separate; never relabel them to populate this pilot.

Review each sport once at 100 resolved selected events and at least 95% resolution
coverage, or the fixed January 19 deadline, whichever comes first. If the target
is unmet, report insufficient evidence. Before that, report descriptive coverage,
data failures and results without tuning the rule or repeated significance tests.
At review show event-level uncertainty, net units, drawdown, sport/market/book
coverage, benchmark dependence, cost stress and all unresolved cases. No claim of
validated profitability follows automatically from this small pilot. Any changed
rule needs a new version and new prospective sample.

This registration changes no phone cards, frozen model, collector cadence, quota
budget or execution capability. The user may still receive supported exploratory
leans outside this stricter research cohort, clearly labeled as such.
